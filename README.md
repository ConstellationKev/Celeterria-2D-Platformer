# Celeterria

This is my first platformer with Godot. I made a semi-difficult obstacle course featuring animated players, egregiously hard spike placements, and lots of 8-bit fun\! I made sure to add a lot of different areas and themes within the game, allowing the user to traverse between environments.

# About

This platformer game is a difficult obstacle course in which a zombie apocalypse survivor needs to get through to get to safety. Along the way, there are deadly traps and spikes that the survivor needs to avoid. They are tasked to travel through countless different terrains as well as environments and meet different kinds of hurdles to overcome. However, there is one catch. They only have one life. If they touch any of the spikes, they are dead and have to restart all the way over to the very beginning.

Expect a very fun but also rage inducing gaming experience with this platformer\! Not many people have completed the course; can you be the next?

# How to play

You can either use arrow keys or WASD and space to control the player. Avoid all spikes and dangerous objects. Do not jump into the “void,” there will be spikes that kill the player on the bottom.
